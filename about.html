Transference maps psychoanalysis as a set of inheritances: the schools that grew out of one another, and the analysts who lay down on one another's couches.

Schools traces the major traditions, what each changed, and what each says heals. Analysts follows the people, placed by birth year, linked by documented analyses. The Index lists everyone on the map.

How to read the lines

Analyzed by: a person's analysts, and their analysts, back up the line
Analyzed: the people someone analyzed
Trained or supervised, including Freud's first circle, who learned from him without a formal analysis

A line means an analysis is documented in print. Many were brief, informal or broken off, and many people had several analysts in turn; where they did, all are shown.

Who is missing

The map reflects how the field kept its records, as much as who was there. Women's analyses were often noted only in passing, and Black and non-European analysts were frequently kept out of institutes or left out of their histories. Where an analyst is unknown, the line simply stops.
